# Website update v5

This version rebuilds the homepage to match the approved visual mock-up.

## Layout changes

- Light cream navigation bar with a simple terracotta JM monogram.
- Full-width research artwork used as the hero background.
- Left-aligned name, position, affiliation, research title, description, and buttons.
- Guaranteed line wrapping in the research description.
- Profile links displayed as five overlapping icon cards.
- Centred “Research focus” section with three illustrated cards.
- Centred “Selected publications” section with three equal-height cards.
- Warm peach footer with three aligned information areas.
- Responsive layout for tablets and phones.
